General Application

Follow these standard guidelines for optimal results across our product range.

1

Mix the Solution

Mix 1 kg of powder in a clean vessel with 1500-1800 ml water.

2

Rest & Apply

Rest for 30 mins, mix again, and apply on a wet wall using a brush, roller, or spray.

3

Dry & Cure

Wait >6 hours before the second coat. Water curing is suggested for best results.
